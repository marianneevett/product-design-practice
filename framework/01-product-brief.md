# Product Brief

A lightweight starting point for turning an ambiguous idea, request, or opportunity into a product problem that can be understood and worked on.

This isn't meant to document everything upfront. Its job is to establish enough shared context to begin making good decisions.

---

## The opportunity

**What are we trying to change?**

Describe the opportunity in plain language. Focus on the situation or behavior that should be different rather than the solution you already have in mind.

> *Example: Customers frequently abandon a task because they don't understand what happens after they submit it.*
